Bunker Run v0.4.0 is live at the same link, so reload the page. I checked the code for errors, but I haven't played it on a phone, so tell me if anything looks or feels wrong.
Reload sounds
Each reload now plays a click when the magazine comes out, a thunk when the new one goes in, and a two-click bolt rack at the end.
The shotgun plays shell-loading ticks instead of the magazine sounds.
7 weapons (tap SWAP to cycle through them)
Pistol has unlimited reserve ammo.
Shotgun is slow and strong up close.
M4 is an assault rifle.
Vector is an SMG with a very high fire rate and a red-dot sight.
AK-47 hits harder per bullet, with wood furniture and a curved magazine.
P90 holds 50 rounds and has a see-through magazine on top.
MP5 is a slim SMG with a forward grip.
Each weapon has its own look, magazine size, reload time and sound. Ammo pickups now refill all of them.
The fast guns charge your Shockwave special more slowly per hit, so it can't be spammed.
Humanoid monsters
The enemies now have heads, torsos, arms and legs, with glowing red eyes. Their legs swing when they walk.
Grunts are green, runners are pale and lean, shooters are armored and carry a gun, and tanks are big purple brutes.
The MONSTERS button in the top-right switches between HUMANOID and BLOCK. The game remembers your choice next time you open it.
On a computer, keys 1–7 pick a weapon, R reloads, E uses the special, and H toggles the monster style.
