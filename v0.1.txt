I built a first version called Bunker Run. It's a small first-person shooter you play in your phone's browser, with nothing to install. It draws at a low resolution, so your Tecno Spark 5 Air should handle it. It's untested on a real phone, so tell me if it lags.

How to play:

Drag your thumb on the left side of the screen to move.
Drag on the right side to look around.
Tap the red FIRE button to shoot.
There are 6 guards in the bunker. Clear them all before your health runs out.

I made assumptions about the setting and the controls. This is just a starting point, so we can change whatever you like. Some ideas for the next step:
