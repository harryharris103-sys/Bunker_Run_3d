Bunker Run v0.3.0 

Each weapon now has a magazine: pistol 12 (unlimited reserve), shotgun 6, rifle 30. The HUD shows loaded/reserve, for example 12/∞.
Tap RELOAD, or just keep firing on an empty magazine and it reloads for you. While reloading, the gun dips and tilts, the magazine drops out and a fresh one slides in, and the shotgun pumps.
Ammo pickups now give a bit more: 6 shells and 30 rifle rounds.

Modern weapon look
The guns are redrawn as a slide pistol, a tactical pump shotgun and an assault rifle with an optic, all with cyan accent lights.
The guns bob when you walk and kick back when you fire, with bigger muzzle flashes.
It's all drawn with simple shapes rather than images, so it shouldn't slow your phone.

Special ability: Shockwave

The SPECIAL button fills like a gauge as you attack. Each enemy you hit adds charge, and a kill adds more. A shotgun blast that hits several enemies counts each one once.
At 100% it glows and says READY. Tap it to blast everything within about 7 steps: it damages them and knocks them back. It kills most small enemies and badly hurts tanks.

I chose Shockwave myself, so if you'd prefer something else, like slowing time, a damage boost or a health burst, tell me and I'll swap it in.
